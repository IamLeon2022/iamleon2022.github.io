I am Siyang Lyu (吕思洋). I received my M.S. in Applied Data Science from the University of Southern California in 2025 and my B.S. in E-Commerce from Wuhan University of Technology in 2023. I previously worked as a research assistant at Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo, under the supervision of Prof. Xiaoyu Shen, studying chain-of-thought compression and knowledge distillation for large language models.

My research interests center on efficient reasoning in large language models. My recent work focuses on chain-of-thought compression and distillation, particularly how compression strategies interact with task domains and reasoning regimes. More broadly, I am interested in the following directions:

  • Thought Compression & Distillation: condition-aware reasoning compression (EMNLP ’26), reasoning structure preservation, and effective supervision for distillation.
  • Adaptive Reasoning: task-adaptive reasoning budgets, deciding when further deliberation is useful, and balancing accuracy with computational cost.
  • Efficient Code Reasoning: concise reasoning supervision and execution-guided planning and revision for code generation.
